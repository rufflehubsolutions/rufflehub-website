The Challenge

Real estate businesses invest heavily in portals like 99acres and MagicBricks. However, leads landing in email notifications suffer from an average wait time of 45 minutes. In real estate, the first person to call wins.

The Solution

We build an automated system using Make and Google Cloud:

  • Instant Capture: The system catches new leads the moment they are generated.
  • Smart Sorting: The system matches the lead preferences to your available properties.
  • WhatsApp Delivery: A message is sent directly to your brokers phone in under 3 seconds.

The Challenge

Broad digital advertising brings in lots of leads, but sales teams often waste up to 60% of their time calling junk numbers or people with the wrong budget.

The Solution

We add a smart scoring layer inside your CRM:

  • Data Check: Incoming leads are instantly checked for location and budget signals.
  • Smart Scoring: A smart formula groups leads from hot buyers down to casual browsers.
  • Priority Routing: Hot buyers skip the general line and go straight to your best closers.
