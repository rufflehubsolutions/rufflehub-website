Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2026

Ruffle Hub Solutions ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data you share with us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website (rufflehub.biz) or engage with our consulting, automation, and PropTech services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide high-value technology and growth partnerships for real estate developers:

  • Personal Identification Information: Full name, work email address, phone number, and company name submitted voluntarily via our system audit or lead capture forms.
  • Technical and Usage Data: IP addresses, browser types, device information, operating systems, and website navigation patterns collected automatically through cookies and Google Analytics.

We use the collected information for specific, legitimate business purposes:

3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Infrastructure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. However, to deliver our services, your data interacts with robust industry-standard infrastructure:

These third-party platforms operate under their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for the independent privacy practices of external platforms.

Our website utilizes cookies and similar tracking technologies (such as Google Analytics 4) to analyze trends, administer the website, and track user movements around the site. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

5. Data Security

We implement strict administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal and business data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.
