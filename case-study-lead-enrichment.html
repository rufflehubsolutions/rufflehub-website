The Challenge

Broad digital advertising campaigns across Meta and Google generate high lead volumes, but internal sales teams frequently waste up to 60% of their working hours calling low-intent inquiries, budget-mismatched leads, or spam submissions. This operational drag leaves high-value buyers waiting for attention while telecallers burn time chasing unqualified numbers.

The Architecture & Solution

We deployed an automated intelligence and scoring layer directly inside the RECA CRM ecosystem:

  • Automated Data Enrichment: Incoming lead payloads from ad platforms are instantly scanned and enriched with location signals, budgetary indicators, and configuration intent.
  • Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) Scoring: The system applies a dynamic scoring algorithm to segment every lead into tier levels (Tier 1 high-intent buyers down to Tier 3 casual browsers).
  • Smart Priority Routing: Tier 1 prospects automatically bypass general call queues, triggering an instant dispatch to senior closers with pre-populated buyer briefs.

The Business Impact

Sales teams doubled their daily productivity by focusing 100% of outbound effort strictly on high-intent prospects. Closing efficiency improved by 28%, and unproductive telecalling hours were slashed in half.
